Join notes

PAYMENTS → INVOICES: Raw gateway notes_invoice_ref has 3 formats — INV-2025-000001, inv-2025-000001, INV2025000002. Normalise to uppercase + dashes before joining to invoice_id.
PLAN → CITIES (dashed): Comparison reference only — not a foreign key. Plan stores "Bangalore"; all other tables store "Bengaluru". Map before comparing.
BOOKINGS — two source grains: Legacy files are one row per test; Nova files are one row per booking (tests nested). The entity above is the normalised model after flattening both sources.
Amount units: PAYMENTS and SETTLEMENTS store paise; INVOICES stores rupees (÷100 to reconcile).
COLLECTIONS is a subset of BOOKINGS: Only bookings where a sample was taken appear here — cancelled and no-show bookings have no collection row.
RESULTS has no PK column: Composite key is (booking_id, test_code).
